iLicense

A mobile game that walks players through the Philippine driver’s licensing process.

I led development of this team capstone at Southern Luzon State University. Players move from student permit to non-professional license, including a written exam and practical driving.

  1. Student permitWhere the licensing pathway begins.
  2. 31-day waitThe app explains why the law requires the permit to mature before a full license.
  3. Non-professional licensePlayers return to the LTO office to apply, and the app includes a written exam and practical driving.
